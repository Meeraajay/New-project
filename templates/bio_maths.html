Bio-Mathematics

Combining science and mathematics for future innovators

Course Overview

The Bio-Mathematics stream combines Biology, Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry to prepare students for careers in medicine, engineering, biotechnology and scientific research. The course develops analytical thinking and scientific skills essential for competitive examinations and higher studies.

Main Subjects

Biology
Mathematics
Physics
Chemistry

Environmental Science

Facilities

Students have access to advanced science laboratories, smart classrooms, research activities, science exhibitions, medical entrance coaching and interactive learning sessions guided by experienced faculty members.

Eligibility Criteria

Students applying for the Bio-Mathematics stream should have successfully completed 10th standard with good marks in Science and Mathematics.

Career Opportunities

The Bio-Mathematics stream opens pathways to careers in Medicine, Engineering, Biotechnology, Pharmacy, Agriculture, Nursing and Scientific Research. Students can also pursue professional courses and higher education in various science-related fields.
