Commerce

Building future leaders in business and finance

Course Overview

The Commerce stream equips students with knowledge in accounting, finance, economics, business studies and entrepreneurship. The course helps students understand the world of trade, banking, taxation and corporate management while preparing them for professional and higher education opportunities.

Main Subjects

Accountancy
Business Studies
Economics
Banking & Finance
Entrepreneurship
Marketing

Facilities

Students are provided with smart classrooms, commerce labs, business workshops, seminars, entrepreneurship training and financial literacy programs conducted by industry professionals and faculty experts.

Eligibility Criteria

Students applying for the Commerce stream should have successfully completed 10th standard with good performance in Social Science and Mathematics.

Career Opportunities

Commerce students can pursue careers in Accounting, Banking, Finance, Business Management, Marketing, Chartered Accountancy, Company Secretaryship and Entrepreneurship. The course also prepares students for business-related higher studies and competitive exams.
