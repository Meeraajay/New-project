Humanities

Understanding society, culture and human values

Course Overview

The Humanities stream focuses on the study of society, history, politics, literature and human behavior. It helps students develop communication skills, critical thinking, creativity and social awareness, preparing them for diverse careers in public service, media, education and research.

Main Subjects

History
Political Science
Sociology
Psychology
Literature
Philosophy

Facilities

Students are provided with interactive classrooms, seminars, debates, cultural activities, research projects, social awareness programs and access to a rich library with humanities and literature resources.

Eligibility Criteria

Students applying for the Humanities stream should have successfully completed 10th standard with good performance in Social Science subjects.

Career Opportunities

Humanities students can pursue careers in Journalism, Law, Civil Services, Teaching, Psychology, Social Work, Public Administration, Writing, Media and Research. The stream also offers excellent opportunities for higher education in arts and social sciences.
