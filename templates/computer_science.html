Course Overview

The Computer Science stream introduces students to programming, software development, artificial intelligence, databases, networking and modern technologies. Students gain practical and theoretical knowledge required for higher studies and careers in the IT industry.

Main Subjects

Python Programming
Database Management
Artificial Intelligence
Web Development
Networking
Cyber Security

Facilities

Our department provides modern computer labs, high-speed internet access, smart classrooms, project guidance, coding workshops and technical seminars conducted by industry experts.

Eligibility Criteria

Students applying for the Computer Science stream should have successfully completed 10th standard with strong performance in Science and Mathematics.

Career Opportunities

Students can pursue careers as Software Developers, Data Analysts, AI Engineers, Cyber Security Experts, Web Developers and System Administrators. The course also prepares students for engineering and IT-related higher education.
